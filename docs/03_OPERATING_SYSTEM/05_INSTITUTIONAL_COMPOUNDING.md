# Institutional Compounding

Every completed mission should improve the next mission.

## Capture without derailment

Valuable ideas often appear while another task is active. Capture the germination in a durable Markdown note, record why it matters and what should happen next, then return to the underlying job. Do not organize all day instead of producing.

## Hub before spoke

The first destination may be a semi-organized hub. Final classification can wait until the idea has enough substance to justify a spoke. Custody comes before perfect architecture.

## Promotion ladder

```text
observation
  -> captured idea
  -> tested method
  -> reusable guide
  -> ratified procedure
  -> institutional default
```

## Evidence standard

Do not canonize an attractive theory merely because it is fluent. Record the field case, the mechanism, the result, known limitations, and the human judgment that made the method useful.
