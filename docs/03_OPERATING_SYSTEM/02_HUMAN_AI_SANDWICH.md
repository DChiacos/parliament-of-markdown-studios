# Human-AI Sandwich

The Human-AI Sandwich prevents agents from producing the middle before the starting world and destination are understood.

## Bottom slice: starting state

The bottom slice contains:

- the real problem and why it matters;
- current files, systems, and versions;
- direct evidence and governing sources;
- decisions already frozen;
- defects, missing pieces, dependencies, risks, and exclusions;
- authority and actions the production crew may not invent.

Discovery may inspect the state. It must not silently mutate it.

## Top slice: end state

The top slice contains:

- observable deliverables and destinations;
- counts, formats, paths, specimens, and status labels;
- objective acceptance tests;
- taste decisions reserved for the human;
- non-goals and publication boundaries;
- the exact review packet expected at completion.

## Middle: bounded production

Only after both slices are explicit can an agentic production pass begin. The middle may contain research, drafting, design, technical work, review, and revision. It may not redefine the bread.

## Readiness test

PASS requires resolvable sources, observable start and end states, explicit scope and exclusions, named acceptance gates, and no missing human decision that the production crew would have to invent.
