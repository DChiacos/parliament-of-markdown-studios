# MarkDown Studios Public Constitution

## Preamble

MarkDown Studios exists to help people remain the artist in the age of AI. Technology should amplify human judgment, not replace it. The studio documents and refines systems that help humans think clearly, create effectively, and direct AI without surrendering agency.

## Article I: Institutional compounding

Every completed mission should leave the institution stronger than before. A task can produce both its immediate result and a reusable method, safeguard, template, or lesson.

## Article II: Human judgment and delegated execution

Human judgment is sovereign. Execution should be delegated when the remaining decisions are bounded, inspectable, and reversible. Consequential choices, identity use, publication, and final approval remain human gates.

## Article III: Remain the artist

AI may generate, compare, draft, and accelerate. The human remains responsible for meaning, taste, direction, and accountability.

## Article IV: Assets compound

Chats are temporary working surfaces. Durable knowledge belongs in inspectable files with ownership, version, status, and provenance.

## Article V: Execution before optimization

When delivery is waiting, improve the work before improving the factory. Architecture must serve production rather than replace it.

## Article VI: Canon before cosmetics

Truth precedes presentation. The controlling source evolves; downstream expressions follow it or declare themselves superseded.

## Article VII: Governed by states

Authority comes from status and evidence, not from a convincing filename or folder location.

## Article VIII: Earn the claim

Vision may describe a trajectory. Public identity may claim only what completed work and inspectable evidence can support.

## Articles IX to XVIII: field-test articles

The articles below entered the private Constitution after the first public release. They hold **candidate / field-test** status: observed in live work and in use, but not yet ratified. They are published here because they shape how the system currently runs, not because they are settled law. Public article numbers follow this public text and do not match the private numbering.

## Article IX: The human pulls the reins

AI expands the possibility space; humans collapse it into direction and action. Expansion without convergence produces possibility without execution. Convergence without expansion produces execution without discovery.

## Article X: Mission before machinery

The institution exists to accomplish real missions, not to produce documents, frameworks, or process for their own sake. A document has value only if it improves execution. Build only what the next confirmed gate requires.

## Article XI: Doctrine is discovered

A principle earns constitutional force only after repeated contact with reality shows that it improves judgment, execution, or outcomes. Observation, reflection, repetition and validation come before doctrine. Experience outranks elegance.

## Article XII: Operational completion

A mission is complete only when the requested end state has been achieved, verified, and accurately reported. Creating, filing, verifying and reporting are four separate events. Work produced but not delivered is unfinished work, and a failed delivery is reported plainly.

## Article XIII: Clarify at material forks

If two reasonable interpretations would produce materially different work, ask one focused question before proceeding. If every reasonable interpretation leads to the same work, execute.

## Article XIV: Checkpoint before scale

Before scaling an expensive, repetitive, or consequential operation, validate the smallest representative output for each meaningful variant. A batch multiplies both correct judgment and bad assumptions.

## Article XV: Durable relay

Context that crosses between independent players, human or AI, travels in a durable mission packet that names sender, recipient, objective, context, constraints, destination, completion condition, evidence and return route. The institution must not depend on human copy-paste or one chat's memory.

## Article XVI: Everything starts in IN

Every fresh artifact enters through an intake state (IN) before it may be classified, copied to canon, or moved to OUT. OUT is a claim that classification or processing occurred, so a brief cannot skip an unperformed checkpoint by naming OUT as its destination. Location represents state; it does not manufacture authority.

## Article XVII: Professional acceptance

Technical completion is not proof of professional success. Acceptance is measured against intended use and defined success criteria: completed files, successful API calls or green statuses may still represent failure. Practical test: would a skilled human trust this result in real work?

## Article XVIII: Coupling governs method

Every task is either tightly or loosely coupled, and coupling, not the operator's judgment in the moment, decides whether steps may be reordered, skipped, or renegotiated. In a tightly coupled task a step performed late is not the step performed; the correct response to a missed step is to start over. Where coupling is unknown, treat the task as tightly coupled.

## Ratification standard

1. **Candidate:** observed and recorded.
2. **Stress test:** used repeatedly in live work.
3. **Ratified:** granted authority after evidence and human review.
4. **Superseded:** replaced by a stronger formulation.
5. **Graveyard:** preserved, inactive history.
