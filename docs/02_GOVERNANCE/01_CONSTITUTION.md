# MarkDown Studios Public Constitution

## Preamble

MarkDown Studios exists to help people remain the artist in the age of AI. Technology should amplify human judgment, not replace it. The studio documents and refines systems that help humans think clearly, create effectively, and direct AI without surrendering agency.

## Article I: Institutional compounding

Every completed mission should leave the institution stronger than before. A task can produce both its immediate result and a reusable method, safeguard, template, or lesson.

## Article II: Human judgment and delegated execution

Human judgment is sovereign. Execution should be delegated when the remaining decisions are bounded, inspectable, and reversible. Consequential choices, identity use, publication, and final approval remain human gates.

## Article III: Remain the artist

AI may generate, compare, draft, and accelerate. The human remains responsible for meaning, taste, direction, and accountability.

## Article IV: Assets compound

Chats are temporary working surfaces. Durable knowledge belongs in inspectable files with ownership, version, status, and provenance.

## Article V: Execution before optimization

When delivery is waiting, improve the work before improving the factory. Architecture must serve production rather than replace it.

## Article VI: Canon before cosmetics

Truth precedes presentation. The controlling source evolves; downstream expressions follow it or declare themselves superseded.

## Article VII: Governed by states

Authority comes from status and evidence, not from a convincing filename or folder location.

## Article VIII: Earn the claim

Vision may describe a trajectory. Public identity may claim only what completed work and inspectable evidence can support.

## Ratification standard

1. **Candidate:** observed and recorded.
2. **Stress test:** used repeatedly in live work.
3. **Ratified:** granted authority after evidence and human review.
4. **Superseded:** replaced by a stronger formulation.
5. **Graveyard:** preserved, inactive history.
